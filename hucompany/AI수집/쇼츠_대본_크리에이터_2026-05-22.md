*   **자막 멘트:** `✅ 신선도 3배↑`, `🥦 재료 낭비 끝!`, `✨ 냉장고 악취 OUT`, `👍 살림 필수템 인정!`
*   **비주얼 연출 팁 (AI 비디오 프롬프트):**
    ```
    A dynamic short video sequence:
    1.  **Problem:** Extreme close-up of withered, brown lettuce leaves in a generic plastic container.
        `Macro lens, f/1.4, detailed texture, natural soft light.`
    2.  **Solution Intro:** A hand elegantly placing a fresh, vibrant strawberry into a sleek, transparent 'automatic vacuum sealing container'.
        `Professional studio lighting, high-key, soft diffused light. Shot with 85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field, creamy background blur.`
    ```
